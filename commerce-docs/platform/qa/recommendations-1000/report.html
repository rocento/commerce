What changed

Conversational recommendation wording now preserves the full product and filter request. Attribute questions accept more polite forms. Whole option phrases such as “rose gold” no longer conflict with a contained “gold” match. Product names also participate in category fallback checks, so milk in the Dairy category remains eligible. Allergy and medical-treatment requirements remain subject to evidence checks.

What the result means: These 1,000 English phrases passed specific checks; this is not a guarantee for every possible customer message. The response tests use a controlled merchant catalog. Thirty-six context-dependent follow-ups correctly ask for context, and forty unsupported evidence claims require clarification. Live catalog checks are listed separately below. The corpus includes template combinations and was used during debugging; its split labels do not represent a blind holdout.
